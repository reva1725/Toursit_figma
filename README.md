# 🌿 TrailSense – AI-Powered Sustainable Tourism Companion

> **Travel Better. Leave a Positive Impact.**

TrailSense is an AI-powered mobile application designed to promote sustainable tourism by helping travelers discover eco-friendly destinations, avoid overcrowded locations, support local communities, and reduce their environmental impact. The platform combines intelligent recommendations, real-time travel insights, and sustainability metrics to create responsible and meaningful travel experiences.

---

## 📱 Features

- 🤖 AI-curated destination recommendations
- 🌍 Sustainable travel suggestions based on user preferences
- 📊 Real-time crowd density indicators
- 🌱 Eco-impact score for destinations and activities
- 🗺️ Interactive map with eco-sensitive zone overlays
- 📅 AI-powered sustainable itinerary builder
- 🏡 Local businesses & artisan directory
- 🚆 Carbon footprint estimator for transportation
- 📖 Trip journal with photo logging
- 📈 Personal sustainability impact tracker
- 🔔 Crowd alerts, weather updates & local event notifications
- 💾 Offline itinerary and map support
- 🌐 Multilingual travel experience
- 🛡️ WCAG 2.1 AA accessible interface
- 🌙 Light & Dark Mode support

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Popular tourist destinations often experience overtourism, environmental degradation, and loss of local culture, while many hidden destinations remain unexplored. Travelers also lack tools to make sustainable decisions during their journey.

TrailSense addresses these challenges by using AI to recommend responsible travel experiences while encouraging support for local communities and reducing environmental impact.

---

## 💡 Solution

TrailSense provides an intelligent travel companion that helps users:

- Discover less crowded destinations
- Reduce carbon emissions
- Build eco-friendly itineraries
- Support local artisans and businesses
- Receive environmental and crowd alerts
- Track their positive travel impact
